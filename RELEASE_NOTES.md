## Undertone 1.2.0

An endless ambient soundtrack, generated locally on your Mac.

- Icon tabs for Studio, Library and Settings.
- Language and optional analytics controls in Settings.
- Optional launch at login and playback of your last composition on launch.
- Customizable global shortcuts for Play/Pause and Next Composition.
- Developer contact and email-copy controls.
- The player opens automatically on a fresh installation.
- Unused atmosphere recordings removed from the app bundle without re-encoding
  the remaining audio.

**Compatibility:** macOS 14+, Apple Silicon and Intel.

**Install:** download `Undertone-1.2.0.dmg`, open it, and drag Undertone into
Applications. Click the wave in the menu bar to open the player.

**Update:** quit the old version before replacing it in Applications.
Your library and preferences are retained.

The DMG is Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. A SHA-256 checksum is
provided as a separate release asset.
